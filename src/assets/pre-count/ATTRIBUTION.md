# Pre-count voice samples

`one.wav`, `two.wav`, `three.wav` and `four.wav` are the last four words ("4, 3, 2, 1") of
["Countdown numbers 20-1 60bpm.wav" by Carmelious](https://freesound.org/people/Carmelious/sounds/620655/),
released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Changes: converted to mono WAV, split into individual words and trimmed with short fades.
